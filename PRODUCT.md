## Platform

web

## Users

Two co-important user groups, weighted slightly toward the first:

- **Commercial & industrial real estate buyers and sellers** — business owners, investors, and operators (gas stations, hotels/motels, restaurants, daycares, self-storage, farms/ALR land, and similar categories) across Surrey and the Lower Mainland, BC. Emphasized slightly over residential because genuinely serious commercial-specialist realtor sites are rare in this market — most competitors are residential-only — making this Manan's differentiated position.
- **Residential home buyers and sellers** (including first-time buyers) across the same geography. Still a core, equally-served audience — just not the site's differentiating edge.

## Product Purpose

A lead-generation and credibility site for Manan Bhullar, a REALTOR® running a dual residential + commercial/industrial practice. Success is a visitor actually making contact (form submission, text, or call) — not just browsing. Every lead-capture point on the site was audited and repaired this session specifically because the mechanics of contact (working forms, sensible CTAs) were found to matter more than any visual polish.

## Positioning

Few agents serving Surrey and the Lower Mainland run a genuinely serious commercial & industrial practice alongside residential — most competitor sites in this market are purely residential. Manan's dual-market fluency, paired with a BBA in Marketing from SFU's Beedie School of Business, is a claim a neighboring residential-only agent's site could not truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

Static multi-page brochure/lead-gen site, no live MLS feed. "Search Properties" deep-links to an external listings search (JS-built URL, opens in a new tab) — the destination is never named in visible copy per a standing client rule. All consultation/inquiry forms submit via Formspree. No hosting connected yet (planned: GitHub → Cloudflare Pages). Content is generated by a Python script (`build.py`) except `index.html`, which is hand-maintained separately and must be manually kept in sync with the shared nav/footer partials (`assets/_nav.html`, `assets/_footer.html`) — this has caused real bugs before.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Plain HTML/CSS/JS: one `assets/style.css`, one `assets/site.js`, a Python static generator (`build.py`). No framework, no npm build step for the site itself. (Node/npm was installed this session only to run the Impeccable design tool — it is not part of the site's own stack.)
- 138+ pages: homepage, buyer/seller flows, 16 commercial category pages, ~99 community/area pages, an early-stage blog (2 posts so far, ~16 more planned), legal stub pages.
- Hard content rules that must always be preserved:
  - "Coldwell Banker Universe Realty" appears exactly once site-wide (homepage footer copyright line only).
  - "Realtor.ca" must never appear in visible copy, even though search functionally deep-links there.
  - Two specific watermarked/copyrighted reference photos are permanently banned, regardless of how they're re-uploaded or re-cropped.
  - No fabricated statistics — every number is either verified or explicitly caveated as unavailable.
  - Never reproduce competitor reference-site content verbatim (names, tenure claims, testimonials, phone numbers, pricing figures) — those sites were used for structural/layout inspiration only.
- Established design tokens (not to be casually changed): off-white paper `#FAFAF8`, ink `#121212`, single accent blue `#1E5FD9` / accent-deep `#164FB8`, General Sans everywhere, no monospace font anywhere (explicitly removed site-wide per client request).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Manan Bhullar. Nav tagline: "REALTOR® | Marketing Specialist (SFU)".
- Verified facts only: BBA in Marketing, Beedie School of Business, SFU; REALTOR® with Coldwell Banker Universe Realty; practice spans residential (Surrey + Lower Mainland) and commercial/industrial; a real but unquantified record-breaking price-per-sqft sale in Fleetwood, Surrey (no exact figure available — never invent one).

- Logo: cursive signature mark — light-gray version for dark backgrounds, black version for the light nav. Favicon is a separate simple "MB" monogram, kept deliberately for legibility at small sizes.

## Product Principles

1. Commercial/industrial capability is the differentiating specialty to lean into — most competing sites in this market are residential-only.
2. Every claim and number must be real and verifiable; when data isn't available at the needed granularity, say so rather than inventing it.
3. Lower the commitment required to make first contact — prefer low-friction paths (text, forms) over high-friction ones (phone calls), matching the site's "free, no obligation, no pressure" positioning.
4. Never let generated or placeholder content imply something false about a place, a person, or a number.
5. One accent color, one typeface, a restrained palette — consistency over novelty. Deliberate departures need a real reason, not just a fresh idea.
