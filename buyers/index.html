A Clear, Guided Process

Here's what to expect when you buy a home with Manan.

01
Get Pre-Approved Before you start searching, get mortgage pre-approval. Manan connects you with trusted lenders and helps you understand your budget and purchasing power.
02
Define Your Needs A conversation about your must-haves, nice-to-haves, lifestyle preferences, and long-term goals — this shapes your tailored property search.
03
Property Search Access to MLS® listings plus local market insight. Showings are scheduled around your schedule, with honest assessments of every property.
04
Make an Offer A competitive offer with the right conditions to protect your interests, negotiated skillfully on your behalf.
05
Due Diligence Inspections, strata documents, title review — you're guided through every subject so you can move forward with confidence.
06
Close & Move In From final walkthrough to key handover, Manan ensures a smooth completion — and stays available whenever you need him after.
